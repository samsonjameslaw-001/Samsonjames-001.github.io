FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do you handle land matters outside Kisumu?

The practice is based in Kisumu, but appropriate matters from other counties can be considered depending on the nature of the work and professional authorization.

What should I bring for a land consultation?

Where available, bring the title or lease, official search, sale agreement, identification documents, survey documents, correspondence and any notices or court documents.

Can I enquire about succession?

Yes. Succession enquiries can be assessed and the appropriate legal route and documentation explained, subject to the applicable professional requirements.
