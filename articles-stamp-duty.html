Stamp Duty in Land Transactions: A Practical Introduction

Stamp duty is an important part of many land transactions in Kenya. The amount and process depend on the nature and value of the transaction and the applicable law. A conveyancing transaction should therefore account for valuation, assessment, payment and evidence of payment as part of the completion process. Parties should also ensure that the transfer documents and supporting requirements are properly prepared. Because rates, procedures and exemptions can change, a specific transaction should be checked against the law and current official requirements before payment or completion.
