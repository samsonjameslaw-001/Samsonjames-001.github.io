Buying Land in Kenya: Key Due Diligence Steps

Buying land is a transaction where careful due diligence should come before payment. A buyer should establish the identity and authority of the registered proprietor, confirm the title particulars and investigate whether the property is affected by a charge, caution, restriction, lease or other encumbrance. The appropriate official searches and supporting documents should be reviewed before the transaction proceeds. The transaction may also require survey and boundary verification, consideration of planning or land-use issues, and appropriate contractual protections. The exact checks depend on the property and the nature of the transaction. This article is general information and is not a substitute for advice on a specific transaction.
