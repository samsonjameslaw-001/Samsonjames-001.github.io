What Happens to Land When a Registered Owner Dies?

When a registered landowner dies, beneficiaries generally need to address the deceased's estate through the succession process before the property can be dealt with in the appropriate manner. The exact procedure depends on whether the deceased left a valid will, the nature of the estate and the circumstances of the beneficiaries. Relevant documents can include the death certificate, identification documents, evidence of assets and the documents required for the succession process. Beneficiaries should obtain appropriate legal advice before selling, transferring or otherwise dealing with estate property.
